The font author, [Illuminati Rex](https://www.illuminatirex.com/ciphers/), describes these characters as "… based on the 1776 Bavarian Illuminati Dirigens or Scottish Knight cipher found in the Secret State Archives Prussian Cultural Center in Berlin."

Due to its age and the way information spread at the time, there are many variations of the characters.

Use "&#x2A;" for the symbol <span class="illuminati">&#x2A;</span>, which means "Order" and was never written in full.
